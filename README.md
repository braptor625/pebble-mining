# Pebble Miner

A mouse-only incremental mining game inspired by *Pixel Miner* on the original Pebble smartwatch.

**Play:** open `index.html` in any browser (no build step, no dependencies). Progress saves automatically in your browser.

## How to play
- Your miner digs to the right on their own. Click or hold on the screen to dig faster.
- **Equipment**: each new tool takes you one layer deeper and doubles your digging. Each world has 8 unique tools; owning all 8 lets you buy the portal to the next world.
- A new world resets your tools and pixels. **Multipliers** carry over, and every world raises their level caps.
- Dig into the **Treasure** buried in your layer, then sell it. Fill the collection: 5 treasures in each of the 7 worlds.
- Click the golden pixel when it appears for a frenzy or a pixel bonus.
