# Pebble Miner

A mouse-only incremental mining game inspired by *Pixel Miner* on the original Pebble smartwatch.

**Play:** open `index.html` in any browser (no build step, no dependencies). Progress saves automatically in your browser.

## How to play
- Your miner digs on their own, layer by layer. Click or hold on the screen to dig faster.
- **Equipment** lets you dig deeper (start on foot with a shovel, work up to drill vehicles). **Multipliers** dig quicker. Sell the **Treasure** you dig up.
- Seven worlds, one every 100 layers, each with its own ground and treasures.
