# Pebble Miner

A mouse-only incremental mining game inspired by *Pixel Miner* on the original Pebble smartwatch.

**Play:** open `index.html` in any browser (no build step, no dependencies). Progress saves automatically in your browser.

## How to play
- Click or hold on the mine: your miner digs toward wherever you point.
- Ore pays out instantly. Spend it in the **Shop** on a better pickaxe, faster swings, ore luck, a refinery, an auto-drill and mining robots (idle income, also while you're away).
- Break the **portal** at the bottom of each world to unlock the next — 7 worlds, each with its own ores.
- Fill the **Codex** by discovering every ore for permanent value bonuses.
- **Reforge** to reset for Ancient Shards, a permanent multiplier on damage and ore value.
- Dynamite (bought or found in treasure chests) blasts a 3×3 area.
